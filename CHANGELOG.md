# Changelog

Notable user-facing changes are recorded here. Found & Made follows semantic
versioning while the public API and deployment contract mature.

## [0.1.0-alpha.0] - 2026-08-25

Initial public beta candidate:

- private-by-default recipe library with manual editing, revisions, variants,
  scaling, and three cooking projections;
- local Owner authentication, invitations, collaboration roles, and optional
  Google sign-in;
- private imports from text, websites, media, PDFs, and supported recipe-manager
  exports;
- discovery, PWA/offline cooking, personal notes, ratings, and history;
- public read-only recipes and collections with approved projections;
- individual recipe PDFs and reusable multi-layout cookbook projects;
- portable exports, checked backup/restore, and optional read-only Hermes MCP;
- non-root multi-architecture container and initial Unraid Community Apps
  template.
